Quake 1.06 shareware data lives here as pak0.pak (id Software, freely
downloadable). It is the WinQuake demo: Episode 1 plus demo1.dem, demo2.dem
and demo3.dem.

  cd sw/quake-riscv && make shareware

Do not vendor the registered pak1.pak. pak0.pak is gitignored (~18 MiB).
On case-insensitive volumes, this file is DATA.txt so it does not collide
with the shareware readme.
