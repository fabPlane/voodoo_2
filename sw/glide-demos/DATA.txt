Official publisher demos of DOS-era 3dfx / Glide titles.

These are freely distributed demos, not the registered games. Full retail
dumps stay off this tree.

  cd sw/glide-demos && ./fetch.sh

Archives land in archives/ and are gitignored. Source is archive.org
direct /download/<id>/<file> URLs.
